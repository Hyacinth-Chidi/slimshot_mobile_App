# Text to speech — a voice from the texts on the timeline

Date: 2026-11-10. Status: design, awaiting the owner's approval. Three codebases:
`slimshot_server` (NestJS + Prisma + BullMQ), `slimshot-admin` (Next.js dashboard) and
`slimshotai` (the Flutter app). Built in that order.

## Intent

SlimShot's users make short videos for Status, TikTok and Reels, and many never talk to camera:
the words on screen carry the video. CapCut lets those words speak — pick a text, pick a voice,
and the narration lands on the timeline. SlimShot has no way to do that. This adds it: **a text
on the timeline becomes a voice clip**, generated by xAI's text-to-speech through SlimShot's
server, paid for in credits like Auto captions, with the voices curated by the owner in the
dashboard.

It is built from what is already there: the server's provider system (which already talks to
xAI for Auto captions), its credits and pricing (versioned per feature), the dashboard's
Providers and Pricing pages, and in the app the sign-in door, the silent price check and
shortfall flow of Auto captions, the audio lanes, and the draft's own audio folder.

## Goals

- From a selected text, **Text to speech**: choose a voice, generate, and the spoken text lands on
  an audio lane at the text's start. **Apply to all texts** does every plain text in one go.
- The voice is an ordinary audio clip: moved, trimmed, turned down, copied, deleted, saved with
  the draft, played identically in the preview and the export (it is a file).
- Voices are browsed for free: each has a sample the owner made once in the dashboard.
- Its own price, separate from Auto captions, set and versioned on the dashboard's Pricing page,
  charged by characters, refunded when the generation fails.
- The owner controls it all from the dashboard: the xAI key (Providers), the price (Pricing),
  which voices are offered and in what order (a new **Voices** page).

## Non-goals (first version)

Voice cloning (xAI supports it; later). A language picker (xAI is sent `auto`). Speed, pitch or
expression controls, and xAI's speech tags (`[pause]`, `<whisper>`) beyond what a user happens to
type. Re-generating a voice automatically when its text is edited. Re-timing texts to their
voices. Linking a text's moves to its voice clip. Changing the voice of existing audio (speech to
speech). Any on-phone text-to-speech engine.

## Anti-goals — failures even if it works

- Browsing voices costing credits, or the app working out a price itself.
- The xAI key reaching the phone in any form.
- A voice that sounds different in the preview and the file (it is one MP3, played by both).
- A failure said in the engine's or the provider's words (`userMessageFor`, plain lines only).
- A charge without a voice, or a voice charged twice on a retry (idempotency keys, as captions).

## What the owner decided

- The tool sits in the editor; the audience makes videos without talking to camera.
- **xAI** generates the voices ("xspace api" in conversation).
- **Its own credit price, like Auto captions**, set in the dashboard.
- Server work, dashboard pages and the app, all done here; the voices get a page of their own or
  share the captions' pages, the builder's call within this design.

## What the builder decided, for the owner to check (from "proceed")

1. **One voice clip per text, CapCut's way**, with Apply to all texts. A script pasted into one
   text is one narration; a script typed line by line is one clip per line. CapCut's own text to
   speech places each clip at its text's start and leaves timing to the user — checked, not
   assumed (sources at the end); this does the same.
2. **Priced by characters**: a new pricing mode, *per started block of N characters, at least M
   credits*. Captions are priced by the audio's length; for a voice the text is what exists
   before anything is made, and characters are how speech providers bill. A flat price per
   request stays available.
3. **Captions are not voiced by Apply to all**: a caption is already speech — the likely way a
   caption set exists in this audience's projects is Auto captions run *over* a generated voice.
   A caption can still be voiced on its own, from its own menu.
4. **The text menu's second entry**, after Edit — the feature this audience comes for, by the
   owner's most-used-first rule.
5. **A voice clip gets its own colour and gutter glyph**, as sound effects did (orange): green,
   `AppColors.voiceTrack`, Emerald 500.
6. **Generating again for a text replaces that text's previous voice** (the clip and its file),
   so trying voices does not stack clips. A voice remembers its text by id for this alone.
7. **Text timing is left alone**, as in CapCut. Snapping already pulls a text's end onto its
   voice's end when the user drags it.
8. **No confirmation step**: the price is checked silently and only a shortfall is shown — the
   owner's call for Auto captions (2026-10-06), applied to the same kind of purchase.
9. **Language `auto`**; xAI detects it. English is among its 20 languages; Nigerian English is not
   offered as a voice, and Pidgin is not on the list ("other languages with varying degrees of
   accuracy").

## The server (`slimshot_server`)

### Data

- `ProviderCapability` gains `text_to_speech`. A credential is per (provider, capability), so
  the owner enters the xAI key once more under Text to speech (it may be the same key).
- `CreditFeature` gains `text_to_speech`; the ledger's existing `feature_charge` /
  `feature_refund` carry it.
- `PricingMode` gains `per_characters`, with `PricingRule.blockCharacters` beside the existing
  `blockCredits` and `minCredits`: credits per **started** block of `blockCharacters`, never below
  `minCredits` — `per_second`'s shape in another unit. Text to speech accepts `per_job` and
  `per_characters`; Auto captions keeps its three. A rule for one feature never prices the other.
- `Voice`: `id`, `provider` (`xai`), `providerVoiceId`, `label`, `sortOrder`, `enabled`
  (false when first synced), the sample's stored file and the text it says, timestamps; unique
  on (provider, providerVoiceId).

### Characters

**The server counts and charges; the app only asks.** A character is a Unicode code point of the
trimmed text (JavaScript `[...text].length`; Dart `text.runes.length` in the app, for the quote
only). A request's characters are the sum over its items. Limits, from config: 2,000 characters
an item, 20,000 a request, 100 items a request — far under xAI's 60,000, and a bound on what one
tap can cost.

### Provider

A `TextToSpeechProvider` beside `SpeechToTextProvider`: `synthesize(text, voiceId, apiKey)` →
MP3 bytes, and `listVoices(apiKey)`. The xAI adapter: `POST https://api.x.ai/v1/tts` with
`{ text, voice_id, language: "auto", output_format: { codec: "mp3", sample_rate: 24000,
bit_rate: 128000 } }`, and `GET https://api.x.ai/v1/tts/voices`; key probing and error mapping as
the speech-to-text adapter does (`provider-http`, `provider-error`). The registry gains
`textToSpeechFor(kind)`; only xAI implements it now.

### The app's API — documented in `docs/app-api/text-to-speech.md`

- `GET /voices` — public: the enabled voices in order, `{ id, label, sampleUrl }`. The sample is
  free to play; nothing here is charged. A `sampleUrl` may be short-lived, like a font's download
  address: the app fetches the list when the sheet opens and never stores one.
- `POST /credits/quote` accepts `{ "feature": "text_to_speech", "characters": 420 }` beside the
  captions form (`docs/app-credits-api.md` §11 updated).
- `POST /speech` — signed in, `Idempotency-Key`, JSON `{ voiceId, items: [{ id, text }] }`. Counts,
  prices, charges, queues; `202` → `{ jobId, status, pollAfterMs, charged: { credits, balance } }`.
  The same key answers with the same job (failed included), as `POST /captions` does.
- `GET /speech/:jobId` — `{ status: queued | running | done | failed, items: [{ id,
  durationSeconds }], failure? }`, only to its owner.
- `GET /speech/:jobId/items/:itemId` — the MP3, only to its owner, while the job's files are
  kept.
- Errors, in the house shape: `TTS_UNAVAILABLE` 503 (switched off, no price, no active key, or no
  voice enabled), `INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS` 402 with `details` (needed, balance),
  `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED` 409, `VOICE_NOT_FOUND` 404, `TEXT_TOO_LONG` 413, plus sign-in and
  suspension as captions.

### The job

A BullMQ queue `speech`, its job id from user and key (captions' pattern). The worker
synthesises each item in order and writes `<tmp>/speech/<jobId>/<itemId>.mp3`; its duration is
read from the file for the job view. **All or nothing**: any item failing fails the job and
refunds the whole charge — a half-voiced Apply to all is not something to sell. A sweeper deletes
a job's files `SPEECH_KEEP_MINUTES` (60) after it ends; the app downloads them at once.

### The dashboard's API — documented in `docs/admin-voices-api.md`

Pricing rules and provider credentials already take a feature / capability parameter; they
accept the new values. New: `GET /admin/voices`; `POST /admin/voices/sync` (adds xAI's voices not
yet known, disabled, labelled by their id); `PATCH /admin/voices/:id` (label, enabled);
`PUT /admin/voices/order`; `POST /admin/voices/:id/sample` (generates the sample sentence
through the provider, stores it where the fonts' previews are stored, replaces the old one —
the owner's xAI spend, never credits). Each change is audit-logged like the others.

## The dashboard (`slimshot-admin`)

Read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing (its `AGENTS.md`: this Next.js differs from
what is remembered).

- **Pricing**: a switch between Auto captions and Text to speech; the per-characters mode reads
  "1 credit per 200 characters, at least 1".
- **System › Providers**: a switch between Speech to text and Text to speech; the key form is the
  one there.
- **Voices** (new, beside Fonts): each voice's label (editable), its sample (play; Make sample),
  an on/off switch, drag to order, and **Sync from xAI**. A voice cannot be switched on without a
  sample, so the app never lists a voice it cannot preview.

## The app (`slimshotai`)

- **Shown only in a build with a server address** (`isToolbarToolVisible`, captions' rule), on a
  selected text's menu as **Text to speech**, second, after Edit. On a caption too (one caption).
- **The door is captions'**: `requireAccount` before the sheet opens.
- **The sheet** (`showEditorSheet`, capped at `kEditorSheetPreviewFraction`): the voices from
  `GET /voices` as tiles — name, and a play/stop of its sample (`just_audio`, one at a time, stopped
  when the sheet closes); **Apply to all texts** (`ApplyToAllToggle`, label "Apply to all texts");
  and **Generate**. While it runs the sheet shows the run in place (CaptionRun's shape: a ring,
  Cancel; a failure's plain line with Try again). The voice list is fetched on every opening and
  kept for the session; offline it says "No internet connection".
- **What is sent**: a text is trimmed; an empty one is skipped by Apply to all, and on its own
  leaves Generate off. A text over 2,000 characters is refused in the app before anything is
  sent ("That text is too long for one voice. Split it into shorter texts."), and Apply to all
  past the request's limits says how many texts to voice at once — the server's limits are the
  same numbers, so a request the app sends is never refused for its size.
- **Paying**: the silent quote; a shortfall shows "Needs N credits · You have M" with
  `EarnCreditsBlock`; the charge reaches the pill (`applyBalance`); a failed job says "Your credits
  were returned." The upload key is held by captions' rules (a retry after a lost response must
  not be a second charge), polling follows the server's `pollAfterMs`, three dropped polls in a
  row are ridden out, and it gives up after 10 minutes. **A download that fails after the job is
  done is retried under the same key** — the server answers with the same job while its files
  are kept — so a charged voice is never lost to a dropped connection.
- **The sample player** is the editor's kind of `just_audio` player
  (`handleInterruptions: false`, `handleAudioSessionActivation: false`; a test reads every
  `AudioPlayer(` under `lib/features/video_editor/`), so `PlaybackGuard` still owns the phone's
  audio.
- **The result**: each item downloaded into `drafts/<id>/audio/voice_<itemId>_<ts>.mp3`
  (`DraftFiles.audioDir`, so it is deleted with the draft), and placed through `placeAudioTrack`
  at its text's start, first free lane — **one undo step for the whole generation**. The
  `AudioTrackModel` gains `isVoice` (written only when true) and `voiceForTextId`; drawn in
  `AppColors.voiceTrack` with its own gutter glyph. A text voiced again loses its previous voice
  clip and file in the same step.
- **Then** the voice is audio like any other: the preview's music player and the export's mixer
  already play it. Auto captions with Source *Audio tracks* captions it, which is how a faceless
  video gets word-highlighted captions in time with the voice — no new timing code.
- **Every message is plain**, through `userMessageFor`.

## Deploying

**The privacy policy and Play's Data safety form must change first** (the house rule: anything
new that sends data off the phone). The text a user voices is sent to SlimShot's server and to
xAI to be spoken, and the generated audio is kept on the server for an hour, then deleted. The
policy lives on the website (`slimshotAI_web`, `/privacy`); the form is in Play Console.

The server first (its migration adds the enums, the column and the `voices` table; the owner runs
it on deploy), then the dashboard. The owner then enters the key under Text to speech, syncs and
samples the voices, switches some on and sets a price. Until all of that is done the server
answers `TTS_UNAVAILABLE` and the app's sheet says "Text to speech is unavailable right now". The
app ships last; an older app never sees the feature.
